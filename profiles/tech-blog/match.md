# Technology, Architecture & Policy Deep-Dive Profile

Use this profile for substantive long-form articles, policy updates, industry analyses, and practical opportunities where primary value lies in concrete explanation, career/migration impact, BIM/design workflows, or durable insight rather than a timely headline alone.

## Priority Topics to Retain & Score Higher
1. **Cutting-edge tech & productivity tools**: Practical tool adoption, engineering/workflow deep dives, architecture/system trade-offs, and macro technology trends.
2. **Macro finance & business monetization**: Market reviews, macroeconomic shifts, industry reports, and concrete commercial/monetization logic.
3. **Overseas architecture & remote/BIM opportunities**: Architectural industry employment trends, BIM/computational design workflows, studio operations, and remote/contract/freelance opportunities in the US, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.
4. **US / Canada / Australia / New Zealand visa & immigration policy**: Concrete changes to work permits, skilled migration, employer sponsorship, occupation lists, or policy rules affecting architecture/tech/skilled professionals.

## Noise to Filter Out / Score Lower
- Pure PR press releases, fluff, or promotional announcements without concrete substance.
- Routine funding news / venture capital roundups without broader market or industry insight.
- Pure architectural photo galleries or aesthetic showcases lacking substantive commentary, BIM/workflow detail, or industry/career relevance.
- Surface-level beginner explainers or generic SEO listicles without practical depth.

## Output & IELTS Academic Vocabulary Requirement
- Write summaries and analysis clearly in Chinese (`zh`).
- For selected **English original articles**, append a dedicated section at the end of the summary extracting **3–5 high-frequency IELTS Listening/Reading academic vocabulary items** from the article, each with its meaning in context and **synonyms / paraphrases** (同义替换).
