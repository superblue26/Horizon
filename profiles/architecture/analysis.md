# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether an architecture, urban design, or BIM/AEC industry article provides valuable career insights, remote/freelance opportunities, studio/practice shifts, or innovative design workflows—especially relevant to the US, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand. Score the substance of the article, not brand prestige.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional.** Directly reveals concrete remote/overseas architectural or BIM job/freelance opportunities, major AEC industry shifts, breakthrough computational/BIM workflows, or pivotal market reports in the US/Canada/Australia/NZ.
- **7-8: High value.** Provides substantive industry analysis, sustainable/innovative building practices, firm management insights, or practical career/skill advice for architects and designers.
- **5-6: Useful.** Covers a noteworthy architectural competition, grant, notable built project with concrete design/construction reasoning, or regional industry trend.
- **3-4: Shallow.** Primarily visual project showcases with brief PR text, routine studio awards, or surface-level commentary lacking practical workflow or industry depth.
- **0-2: Noise.** Pure photo galleries, commercial product ads, or off-topic filler.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize actionable career/remote opportunities, BIM/AI workflow adoption in architecture, market demand in target countries (US, CA, AU, NZ), and substantive design innovation. Penalize pure aesthetic fluff without technical or industry context.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
