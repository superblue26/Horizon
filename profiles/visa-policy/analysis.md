# Evaluation goal

Evaluate whether a visa, immigration, or cross-border work policy article provides concrete, actionable, and verifiable policy details affecting skilled professionals (especially architects, BIM/AEC specialists, and tech workers) targeting the US, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Exceptional.** Official or authoritative breakdown of major work visa / skilled migration rule changes, quota updates, wage thresholds, occupation list additions/removals, or processing timelines in the US, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand.
- **7-8: High value.** Detailed analysis of employer sponsorship rules, skill assessment criteria, English requirement updates, or practical pathways for international professionals.
- **5-6: Useful.** Covers regional program draws, notable court/regulatory updates, or comparative policy context with concrete facts.
- **3-4: Shallow.** Vague speculation, repetitive summaries of old rules, or opinion pieces lacking specific regulatory details.
- **0-2: Noise.** Promotional agency marketing, clickbait headlines without substance, or off-topic regions.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize concrete eligibility rules, effective dates, target countries (US, CA, AU, NZ), and direct impact on skilled workers or remote/cross-border employment. Penalize unverified rumors and agency self-promotion.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
