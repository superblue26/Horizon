### [Henning Larsen Reuses an 1883 Norwegian Sawmill for Gard](https://archinect.com/news/article/150576735/henning-larsen-transforms-1883-sawmill-into-marine-workplace) ⭐️ 5.0/10

rss · Archinect \(海外建筑职场与行业\) · Nov 26, 15:33

**「Background」** The Strømsbu Sawmill in Arendal, Norway, founded in 1883, is a waterfront industrial complex that has served as a sawmill, timber outlet, warehouse and car repair shop. Its conversion for marine insurer Gard required Henning Larsen and Ramboll to preserve that layered identity while creating a contemporary office workplace.

**「Solution」** The scheme restores the existing timber warehouse and sawmill, reconstructs a previously demolished intermediate building, and adds a warehouse extension and connecting structure to form three distinct volumes. Reclaimed natural stone from the original framework and foundations is used in new walls, landscape features and drainage; timber logs become terraces and stairs, and reclaimed tiles recall the site’s industrial floors. Working with Ramboll ecologists, the landscape uses native coastal grasses, meadows and nectar plants, plus sand beds, bee hotels and fallen timber to support insects, birds and butterflies.

**「Takeaway」** The project shows how adaptive reuse can pair heritage retention with material recovery and biodiversity goals rather than treating them as separate add-ons. For design teams, the lesson is to integrate salvage and ecological habitats into the earliest spatial and construction decisions.

**「Background」** The Andrew Doolan Best Building in Scotland, the annual RIAS prize named after Scottish architect Andrew Doolan, who died in 2004, recognises projects of any typology — and the 2026 verdict went to a conversion rather than a new build. The Edinburgh Futures Institute occupies a Category A-listed Victorian infirmary originally designed by David Bryce, which had stood vacant since 2003 and was considered at risk of dereliction. Returning a large, heavily protected institutional structure to everyday public and academic use is precisely the design problem the award is testing for.

**「Solution」** Bennetts Associates based the redevelopment on restoring the existing fabric, reopening an original clock tower and public plaza and reinstating the original wards and 150-metre-long corridors. It was chosen ahead of two other adaptive-reuse shortlist entries: the Tarlair Outdoor Pool Pavilion by Studio Octopi and Paisley Central Library by Collective Architecture.

**「Takeaway」** The award shows that heritage conversion pays off when structural and circulation restoration is paired with a clearly programmed public layer, rather than cosmetic conservation alone. It also signals that education clients will back flexible, mixed-use interiors inside protected historic envelopes.

**「Background」** Canada&\#x27;s Express Entry system is now running under a revised Comprehensive Ranking System that came into force on Nov. 3, 2026, and IRCC has continued to tilt 2026 selections toward candidates with provincial nominations, Canadian work experience and French-language proficiency. The Nov. 26 STEM round was the first STEM-specific selection in more than two and a half years; the previous one, on April 11, 2024, issued 4,500 invitations at a CRS cutoff of 491.

**「Solution」** The Nov. 26 draw issued 600 Invitations to Apply to STEM candidates with a minimum CRS score of 547, and only profiles created before 4:52:51 p.m. UTC on Dec. 2, 2025 qualified under the tie-break rule. It was one of 68 Express Entry draws held in 2026, which together produced 158,008 ITAs, led by French-language proficiency \(66,500\) and Canadian Experience Class \(57,750\) selections. Under the new Labour Market Integration Factor, candidates can earn the higher of two scores: 20, 40 or 70 additional points for at least one year of Canadian work experience in a high-wage occupation \(added to existing length-of-employment points, capped at 150, or 140 with a spouse or common-law partner\), or 50, 70 or 100 points for a qualifying job offer that no longer requires a Labour Market Impact Assessment. **「Takeaway」** For architects, BIM/AEC professionals and other skilled workers, the 547 STEM cutoff sits well above the 507–523 range seen in 2026 Canadian Experience Class draws, so most candidates will need strong Canadian experience, a provincial nomination, or the new high-wage job-offer points to be competitive. ### [Canada to Restrict Low-Wage TFWP to In-Demand Occupations from March 2027](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/11/canada-moves-to-restrict-low-wage-tfwp-to-in-demand-occupations-1182920.html) ⭐️ 8.0/10

rss · CIC News \(加拿大工签与移民\) · Nov 26, 22:14

**「Background」** Canada’s Temporary Foreign Worker Program \(TFWP\) low-wage stream has already been tightened through a low-wage moratorium and a wage threshold raised to 120% of the provincial or territorial median wage. Ottawa is now moving toward a more targeted model: ESDC announced on Nov. 6, 2026 that low-wage LMIA processing will be limited to in-demand occupations in provinces and territories that opt in, which ESDC calls “targeted access.”

**「Policy changes」** Effective March 1, 2027, for opted-in jurisdictions, the new occupational restrictions will apply to jobs located in census metropolitan areas \(CMAs\) and paying less than 120% of the median wage in the job’s province or territory; ESDC will process low-wage Labour Market Impact Assessments \(LMIAs\) only for whitelisted occupations. Each whitelist will be a base list of occupations in the Canadian Occupational Projection System \(COPS\), plus occupations a province or territory adds and minus any it removes; ESDC confirmed the base relates to “occupations experiencing labour shortages” but did not specify the COPS time frame, shortage strength, or seasonality treatment. In population centres within large CMAs \(over 250,000\), the existing low-wage moratorium continues—so even whitelisted occupations get no low-wage LMIA if unemployment is 6% or higher unless a priority-sector exemption applies—while in smaller CMAs \(100,000–250,000\) and areas outside population centres in large CMAs, the whitelist supersedes the unemployment-rate moratorium from March 1, 2027. Until then, ESDC continues accepting low-wage LMIAs for all occupations subject to the existing moratorium; through Jan. 7, 2027, 30 CMAs remain covered, and on Nov. 6, 2026 ESDC extended maximum low-wage LMIA duration to two years from one, with the low-wage threshold at $36.92/hour in Ontario and $38.40/hour in British Columbia as of writing, while the 2027–2029 Immigration Levels Plan sets 50,000 TFWP admissions for 2027 \(down from 82,000 as of fall 2025\) and 170,000 IMP workers.

**「Practical impact」** The changes mainly narrow low-wage LMIA access for affected CMA jobs, so applicants should check whether their occupation is on a provincial whitelist and whether pay falls below the 120% median-wage line. For architects, BIM/AEC professionals, and other skilled workers, employer LMIA readiness and the separate, LMIA-free IMP route will remain practical factors to watch.

### [DNA Kits and Canadian Citizenship by Descent: Leads, Not Proof](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/11/a-dna-kit-can-point-you-to-a-canadian-ancestor-is-it-enough-to-claim-citizenship-by-descent-1182909.html) ⭐️ 5.0/10

rss · CIC News \(加拿大工签与移民\) · Nov 26, 18:23

**「Background」** Since Bill C-3 took effect on December 15, 2025, Canadian citizenship can pass down a direct family line with no generational limit for people born before that date, and millions of Americans may qualify without knowing it. DNA kits can help find a Canadian ancestor, but the article stresses that a match alone does not prove citizenship.

**「Policy details」** IRCC does not accept self-arranged DNA results; after an application, it may request a test from an accredited lab to confirm only a parent-child relationship for one generation. Applicants need official records for every generation showing who was whose child and that the anchor ancestor was Canadian, with primary documents supported by secondary records. Quebec birth or marriage certificates issued before January 1, 1994 are not accepted; for older births, BAnQ issues a certified copy of the parish baptism entry. For children born abroad on or after December 15, 2025, if the Canadian parent was also born abroad, that parent needs 1,095 days of physical presence in Canada before the birth.

**「Takeaway」** For architects, BIM/AEC professionals, and skilled workers, DNA relative matching can reveal an ancestry claim but does not create a work or migration pathway. The practical step is to assemble an official documentary chain and consider legal review for complex lines before applying.
