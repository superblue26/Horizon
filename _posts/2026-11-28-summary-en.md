### [Joseph Choma’s Origami-Inspired Foldable Structures in Florida](https://archinect.com/news/article/150576766/joseph-choma-explores-origami-inspired-architecture-in-florida-exhibition) ⭐️ 5.0/10

rss · Archinect \(海外建筑职场与行业\) · Nov 27, 14:33

**「Background」** Joseph Choma, an architect and educator who directs Florida Atlantic University’s School of Architecture and founded Design Topology Lab, is opening a solo exhibition at the Morikami Museum and Japanese Gardens in Delray Beach, Florida. Titled Foldable Structures: Origami x Architecture and opening on December 12, it presents four large-scale folded fiberglass structures along with paper models, drawings, and videos documenting his process.

**「Design Approach」** The outdoor installations, made specifically for the show, test how folding principles can inform architectural structures; Kraken \(2026\), for example, is a sculptural vault derived from a starfish-like two-dimensional crease pattern, with five spiralling legs shaped individually to rest on the ground and subtly varied for an organic appearance. Choma’s approach departs from conventional folded-plate construction by relying on controlled buckling rather than rigid planar elements joined at angles, and closely spaced folds provide structural strength. The Miura Fold—a repeating parallelogram-based crease pattern developed by Japanese astrophysicist Kōryō Miura in 1970—informs the geometry of Kraken’s legs, while the museum frames the work as a step toward flat-packed structures carried to a site and folded by hand into a rigid three-dimensional shelter. Choma previously explored foldable shelters with students at Clemson University, and the exhibition situates his practice at the intersection of folding, mathematics, structure, and material innovation.

**「Takeaway」** For architects and digital-design professionals, the exhibition is less a career listing than a design-method signal: folding logic can connect flat-pack geometry, material behavior, and assembly constraints in one structural system. Its practical relevance lies in treating controlled buckling and repeated crease patterns as parameters to test, document, and translate into prefabricated or on-site assembly.

### [Canada Extends Ebola-Related Immigration and Travel Restrictions to February 2027](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/11/canada-extends-ebola-related-immigration-and-travel-bans-until-february-2027-1182950.html) ⭐️ 5.0/10

rss · CIC News \(加拿大工签与移民\) · Nov 27, 19:03

**「Background」** Canada has kept Ebola-linked border measures in place since May 2026, when document suspensions and arrival health requirements were introduced as one of the first uses of the expanded executive powers created by Bill C-12. The restrictions cover foreign nationals residing in the Democratic Republic of the Congo \(DRC\), Uganda and South Sudan, at a time when the DRC was reporting 12,021 confirmed cases and 5,904 deaths as of Nov. 24 — its 17th outbreak since 1976 — while IRCC data from June 12 showed roughly 36,000 permanent residence applications from residents of the three countries awaiting final decisions.

**「What the Extension Does」** A federal notice released Nov. 27, 2026 extended the entry ban and the suspension of approved visas, permits and electronic travel authorizations for applicants outside Canada who were residing in the DRC, Uganda or South Sudan when they applied, now running until midnight Eastern Time on Feb. 25, 2027, with the full order published in the Nov. 28 Canada Gazette. Foreign nationals who have been in the DRC within the past 21 days remain barred from entering or transiting Canada, and an interim order under the Aeronautics Act, in force since July 20, bars air carriers and private operators from boarding affected foreign nationals on flights to Canada. Canadian citizens and landed permanent residents with recent travel to the three countries may still enter, subject to a mandatory health assessment on arrival plus a 21-day quarantine, or isolation in a medical facility if symptomatic. IRCC continues processing new and existing applications but withholds final decisions until the restrictions lift; affected individuals are notified by email, and suspended documents reactivate automatically. Although the WHO declared Uganda Ebola-free on Aug. 27 and South Sudan has recorded no confirmed cases, the measures apply to all three countries, and non-compliance carries Quarantine Act penalties of up to $1 million in fines, three years&\#x27; imprisonment, or both.

**「Takeaway」** For residents of the DRC, Uganda or South Sudan — including architects, BIM/AEC professionals and other skilled workers — applications will keep moving through processing, but no visa or permit will be finalized before February 2027 at the earliest, and any already-approved document stays suspended until then. Applicants should plan timelines around the Feb. 25, 2027 date rather than expect an earlier release.

The supplied evidence does not substantiate that speedup, and a commenter identifies the post&\#x27;s title as &quot;Supercharging Transformer Residual Connections.&quot; For AI/ML systems practitioners, the relevant claimed target is efficiency, but no benchmark methodology, version, or availability details are supplied.

hackernews · ComputedCamp995 · Nov 27, 23:06 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50672565)

**「Background」** DeepCrossAttention targets a basic building block of transformer models: residual connections, which carry earlier layer outputs forward. Instead of relying on the usual additive path, DCA uses learnable, input-dependent weights and depth-wise cross-attention to combine outputs from previous layers, aiming to avoid the information dilution that occurs as networks deepen. The underlying paper reports lower perplexity for a given parameter budget and training time at a small parameter cost.

**「Scope of the reported gain」** Teams evaluating DCA should read the reported speedup as a training-efficiency result: the paper&\#x27;s claim is up to 3x faster training to reach a target perplexity, plus improved training stability, not a serving-latency win, so it offers no direct inference benefit to already-deployed models. Adopting it is also an architectural change rather than a config flag, replacing fixed additive residual connections with learnable, input-dependent cross-attention routing over Q, K, and V across depth.

**「Community Discussion」** Commenters compared the residual-connection approach to DenseNet and criticized the blog&\#x27;s use of line graphs for non-temporal method comparisons; one also noted the post&\#x27;s title as &quot;Supercharging Transformer Residual Connections.&quot;

### [Show HN: bouncemarket streams a deterministic bouncing-ball prediction market](https://bouncemarket.minhanhday.com/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Developer mday404 has released bouncemarket, a live prediction-market game where players bet on where a server-simulated bouncing ball will land. The full drop is simulated server-side and streamed to clients over server-sent events with a small buffer; each round is deterministic from its starting position and can be replayed exactly, although tiny changes to that position can change the outcome. Rule-based AI traders keep the market moving but drop out of any round with more than five people, so crowded rounds are driven by human players.

hackernews · mday404 · Nov 27, 15:01 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50667726)

**「Background」** Prediction markets let participants buy and sell shares whose payout depends on a future outcome, so the share price acts as a crowd-sourced probability estimate. bouncemarket applies that structure to a server-simulated bouncing ball, with each round deterministic from its starting position and streamed to clients as server-sent events.

**「Impact」** Because a round can be replayed exactly from its starting state, players can analyze past drops, but the sensitivity to tiny starting-position changes means nearby starting positions may not produce a stable outcome pattern to bet on.

**「Community discussion」** Commenters asked for a slower drop, with charlieyu1 saying odds can jump from 0.01 to 0.99 in about a tenth of a second, making betting hard. vova\_hn2 reported that ignoring the ball and buying cheap shares on both sides is an efficient strategy because both can often be bought cheaply, while geoffschmidt argued the game becomes a bot-versus-bot contest once a frontier model is asked to solve the starting-position fraction.
