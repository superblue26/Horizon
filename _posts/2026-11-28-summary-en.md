### [Joseph Choma Brings Origami-Inspired Foldable Structures to a Florida Exhibition](https://archinect.com/news/article/150576766/joseph-choma-explores-origami-inspired-architecture-in-florida-exhibition) ⭐️ 5.0/10

rss · Archinect \(海外建筑职场与行业\) · Nov 27, 14:33

**「Context」** Architect and educator Joseph Choma is opening a solo exhibition, Foldable Structures: Origami x Architecture, at the Morikami Museum and Japanese Gardens in Delray Beach, Florida, on December 12. The show brings together four large-scale folded fiberglass structures alongside paper models, drawings, and videos documenting his design process. Choma is Director of Florida Atlantic University&\#x27;s School of Architecture and founder of Design Topology Lab; he previously explored foldable shelters with students at Clemson University. The exhibition matters because it presents folding as a structural and material strategy rather than only a visual motif.

**「Design Approach」** Choma&\#x27;s approach differs from conventional folded-plate construction by relying on controlled buckling instead of rigid planar elements joined at angles, with closely spaced folds providing structural strength. The Miura Fold, a repeating parallelogram-based crease pattern developed by Japanese astrophysicist Kōryō Miura in 1970, informs the geometry of Kraken&\#x27;s legs. Kraken \(2026\) is a sculptural vault developed from a starfish-like two-dimensional crease pattern, with five spiralling legs individually shaped to rest on the ground and subtle variations intended to give it an organic appearance. The museum frames the work around a future in which an entire architectural structure is flat-packed, carried to a construction site, and folded by hand into a rigid three-dimensional shelter.

**「Practical Significance」** For architects and BIM professionals, the exhibition highlights crease-based geometry, controlled deformation, and flat-pack assembly as design and fabrication constraints rather than decorative effects. However, it is primarily a project showcase and offers limited actionable guidance on career paths, remote work, or market analysis.

### [Canada Extends Ebola-Related Immigration and Travel Bans to February 2027](https://www.cicnews.com/2026/11/canada-extends-ebola-related-immigration-and-travel-bans-until-february-2027-1182950.html) ⭐️ 6.0/10

rss · CIC News \(加拿大工签与移民\) · Nov 27, 19:03

**「Background」** Canada has kept Ebola-related border measures in place since May 2026, when it first suspended immigration documents for foreign nationals residing in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Uganda and South Sudan, drawing on expanded executive powers under Bill C-12 that came into force in March 2026. The DRC remains the centre of the outbreak — 12,021 confirmed cases and 5,904 deaths as of Nov. 24, its 17th outbreak since 1976 — and Ottawa has rolled the restrictions over repeatedly, even though the WHO declared Uganda Ebola-free on Aug. 27 and South Sudan has recorded no confirmed cases to date.

**「Solution」** A federal notice released Nov. 27, 2026 extends the entry ban, document suspension and arrival health requirements until midnight Eastern Time on Feb. 25, 2027 — a further 90 days and the third extension since the measures took effect. Approved visas, permits and eTAs stay suspended for applicants outside Canada who were residing in the DRC, Uganda or South Sudan when they applied; foreign nationals who have been in the DRC within the past 21 days cannot enter or transit Canada, and an interim order under the Aeronautics Act bars air carriers and private operators from boarding them. Citizens and landed permanent residents may still enter after travel to the three countries, subject to a mandatory health assessment on arrival and a 21-day quarantine, or isolation in a medical facility if symptomatic. IRCC continues processing applications but withholds final decisions — roughly 36,000 permanent residence applications from the three countries were awaiting decisions as of June 12 — notifying affected applicants by email and reactivating suspended documents automatically once restrictions lift; non-compliance carries Quarantine Act penalties of up to $1 million in fines, three years&\#x27; imprisonment, or both.

**「Takeaway」** This is a targeted public-health border measure, not a change to skilled migration or work-permit criteria, so applicants residing elsewhere face no new requirements. Architects, BIM/AEC professionals and other skilled workers living in the DRC, Uganda or South Sudan should expect visa or permit issuance and PR finalisation to remain on hold until Feb. 25, 2027, or until the order is lifted.

A security report describes credit-card skimming scripts that abuse WebRTC on popular shopping sites, according to the submitted account. The available material does not name the affected merchants, identify the script families, state how many sites were involved, or explain how the WebRTC channel is used to handle stolen card data, so the campaign&\#x27;s scale and exact mechanism remain unverified. The report frames the issue as a third-party script risk for e-commerce sites.

hackernews · scanr · Nov 27, 15:40 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50668145)

**「Earlier WebRTC skimming report」** Card-skimmer attacks generally work by injecting malicious JavaScript into checkout pages so entered payment data can be exfiltrated. In March 2026, RH-ISAC reported what it called the first observed use of WebRTC as a skimming channel, in a campaign exploiting a PolyShell vulnerability in Magento and Adobe Commerce; the new report concerns similar WebRTC-based exfiltration on other retail sites, not a confirmed continuation of that campaign.

**「Impact」** For e-commerce operators, the reported attack makes third-party JavaScript on storefront and checkout pages a payment-card exposure; the supplied discussion argues that sites embedding such scripts remain vulnerable regardless of whether data is sent over WebRTC or ordinary HTTP requests. Operators would need to inventory and constrain third-party scripts rather than rely on transport-level differences.

**「Community discussion」** Commenters focused less on WebRTC itself than on third-party script trust: afavour argued that the approach could work with simple HTTP requests and that WebRTC is mainly less frequently monitored, while s\_u\_d\_o asked why ad providers do not sandbox ads to prevent client-side JavaScript execution. jszymborski said the report justifies ad blocking.

### [Nerve-connected bionic hand used independently at home in Utah trial](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/11/27/1149946/cutting-edge-prosthetics-allow-researchers-to-probe-the-human-mind/) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Avi Davidson is the ninth participant in a University of Utah clinical trial of a prosthetic hand that connects directly to nerves in his arm, and the first to take it home and use it independently, for tasks such as drinking from a cup and writing. He has electrodes implanted in his arm, with wires exiting the limb to plug the device in, and he describes hundreds of hours of lab training and physical therapy fitted around his job as a trauma therapist. The researchers say the trial is meant to answer longstanding questions: how the technology performs outside the lab, what people actually use it for, and where it can be improved. They also do not yet know whether using the hand will change how his missing left hand is represented in his brain, which was measured in scans before the trial began. Davidson will have the device at least until late January 2027 and potentially another year; how long the hardware can be expected to last in a person&\#x27;s body is unknown, and he will need further surgery to remove the implants and will not keep the prosthetic long term.

rss · MIT Technology Review · Nov 27, 10:00

**「Background」** The University of Utah has been developing its Skywalker-style bionic arm since 2015; the device builds on DEKA&\#x27;s LUKE robotic prosthesis and adds the BIOS direct nerve interface to make it a neuroprosthesis. In September 2026, the university announced the first at-home trial of the arm, with Avi Davidson as the inaugural participant testing it for a year in everyday life. The current report follows that trial as Davidson becomes the first person to use the nerve-connected hand independently at home.

**「Impact」** The trial illustrates the burden such research places on volunteers: Davidson underwent surgery to implant electrodes, faces another operation to remove them, has absorbed hundreds of hours of training and therapy alongside full-time work, and gives the device back at the end. It also surfaced an unexplained effect the team will have to account for — Davidson reports that his prosthetic wrist feels sore and stiff after overuse, much like a flesh-and-blood wrist, and that stretching relieves it.
